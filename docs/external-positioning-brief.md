# EcoXchange External Positioning Brief

**Approved working position · August 2026**

## One-line position

EcoXchange is building the operating layer for investable distributed energy, connecting project-level digital ownership administration, independent production evidence, and PPA-linked pro-rata distribution controls.

## Three complementary capabilities

1. **Digital ownership and capital administration** — permissioned project-interest and cap-table workflows designed to support compliant project-level capital formation.
2. **Independent production evidence** — source-labeled inverter, utility, and weather-modeled inputs that produce a verified, flagged, or pending monthly determination.
3. **PPA-linked pro-rata administration** — modeled distribution eligibility and allocation tied to documented project economics and the production determination.

None of the three capabilities should be presented as the whole company. Verification is the evidence rail and a key differentiator, not the complete product category.

## Current pilot

The controlled pilot may include real project intake, secure data-source planning, a source-labeled production backtest, manual evidence review, digital ownership and cap-table workflow review, and modeled PPA-based pro-rata allocation.

The pilot is non-transactional. It does not open an offering, accept funds or investment commitments, create legal ownership, execute payments, or represent that transfer-agent, custody, accreditation, or secondary-market systems are live.

## Approved audience

- U.S. solar developers and operators with permitted 1–20 MW projects
- Capital, compliance, data, transfer-agent, payment, and infrastructure partners
- Aligned investors and family offices evaluating the model
- Prospective technical and operating collaborators

## Claims requiring additional approval or evidence

Do not claim that EcoXchange currently administers a live securities offering, accepts investments, holds customer funds, creates legal ownership, executes distributions, replaces revenue-grade metering, operates a secondary market, or has verified production for a live customer project. Any project-specific offering, return, fee, timing, tax, payment, or legal claim requires the corresponding authoritative source and approval.

## Partner description

EcoXchange gives project stakeholders one controlled workflow for reviewing project intake, ownership administration, production evidence, and modeled distribution controls. Partners can evaluate the operating model without exposing a live offering or moving funds during the pilot.
