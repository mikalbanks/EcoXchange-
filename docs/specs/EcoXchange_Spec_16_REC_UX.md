## § 1. Purpose

Three surfaces:

1. **Investor disclosure** — what the REC revenue is, whether it's contracted or merchant, and **who owns the green claim**. The last one is mandatory and non-dismissible.
2. **Developer tooling** — intake that captures attribute ownership correctly, a monetization calculator, and the variance report as a business-development artifact.
3. **LOI clause library** — the contract language that makes any of this enforceable.

**§ 4 and § 5 are the highest-leverage work in this entire REC package** and require no engine, no registry, and no counsel. Adding the § 4 intake questions to the developer form is a one-day change that improves every LOI conversation from the next one onward.

The hatch pattern is doing real work. It makes "this number could move" legible at a glance, in a screenshot, in a deck, without reading a legend.

## § 3. Investor Surfaces

### 3.1 Attribute Ownership Disclosure — mandatory

Renders on every offering page and every project dashboard. Above the fold. Not collapsible, not dismissible, not behind a "learn more."

Three variants, driven by `project_rec_enrollment.attribute_ownership`:

**A — SPV retains and sells attributes**

> ### Environmental attributes
> The renewable energy certificates generated by this project are sold to third parties. Investors receive the cash proceeds from those sales as part of project revenue.
>
> **Investors in this offering do not hold, and may not claim, the renewable energy attributes of this generation.** The purchaser of the certificates holds that claim.

**B — Attributes conveyed to the offtaker (bundled PPA)**

> ### Environmental attributes
> The renewable energy certificates generated by this project are conveyed to the power purchaser under the offtake agreement. The project receives no separate certificate revenue, and this is reflected in the projected returns.
>
> **Investors in this offering do not hold, and may not claim, the renewable energy attributes of this generation.**

**C — Attributes retired on investors' behalf** *(future product; do not build the copy path until the product exists)*

> ### Environmental attributes
> Certificates generated by this project are retired on behalf of investors rather than sold. **This reduces cash yield relative to a project that sells its certificates.** Retirement records are available in your project documents.

**Engineering constraints:**

- `attribute_ownership = 'unknown'` renders variant B and raises a build-time warning. **Never render an ambiguous or absent state.**
- Copy strings live in a single locked module. Changes require the same review as PPM language. Add to the CI vocabulary check.

**Why this is non-negotiable:** an investor who believes they hold a carbon claim they do not hold is a greenwashing exposure for EcoXchange and a fiduciary problem for any RIA who ever allocates. It also directly answers Blumberg's barrier #2 — a fiduciary cannot allocate into a black box, and attribute ownership is exactly the kind of thing that is normally opaque.

### 3.2 Yield Decomposition Panel

Replaces the single blended yield figure. Stacked horizontal bar plus a table.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ § REVENUE COMPOSITION                          [IBM Plex]   │
│                                                             │
│ ████████████████████░░░░░░░░░░                              │
│ Energy 58%          RECs 42%                                │
│                                                             │
│ ENERGY SALES          $  XXX,XXX / yr   Contracted (PPA)    │
│ REC REVENUE           $  XXX,XXX / yr                       │
│   ├ Contracted        $  XXX,XXX / yr   [solid #2E7D52]     │
│   └ Merchant          $   XX,XXX / yr   [hatch #8DC4A4]     │
│                                          est. · asof 2026-07-15 │
│                                          source: [name]     │
│ ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────     │
│ CONTRACTED SHARE OF REVENUE                          87%    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`CONTRACTED SHARE OF REVENUE` is the headline number an allocator actually wants. Make it the largest figure on the panel. It is the single metric that tells an underwriter how much of this yield is a promise versus a bet.

**Rules:** never display a blended figure without the decomposition available in the same view. Every merchant figure carries `asof` and source adjacent. If the reference price is stale (>30 days, per Spec 13 § 5.2), the merchant row renders as `—` with "price data stale," not a number.

### 3.3 REC Coverage Indicator

```
§ REC COVERAGE — NEXT 12 MONTHS
████████████████░░░░░░░░   68% contracted forward
                           32% merchant exposure

Contracted through:  Dec 2027
Weighted price:      $XX.XX / REC  [contracted]
Merchant reference:  $XX.XX / REC  [est · asof · source]
```

Only renders when a forward strip exists (Spec 15 § 6). Absent forwards, show 0% contracted rather than hiding the panel — the absence of hedging is information.

### 3.4 Merchant Sensitivity

Scenario table, not a projection.

| Merchant REC price | Modeled cash yield |
|---|---|
| Held flat at reference | X.X% |
| −25% | X.X% |
| −50% | X.X% |
| Zero merchant REC revenue | X.X% |

Header, IBM Plex Mono, mandatory: `MODELED SCENARIOS — NOT FORECASTS. MERCHANT CERTIFICATE PRICES ARE POLICY-DRIVEN AND MAY CHANGE MATERIALLY.`

Include the zero row always. It is the honest floor and the row a serious allocator looks at first.

**Never** render a fitted trend line or a projected future price. See Spec 13 § 5.3.

### 3.5 Production → Certificate Trace

Extends the existing verification panel. Per month: verified kWh → basis applied → carry in/out → forecast certificates → issued certificates → variance.

This is the transparency artifact. It shows an investor the entire chain from photons to certificate revenue with no unexplained step — which is precisely the gap the verification engine exists to close.

## § 4. Developer Surfaces

### 4.1 Intake Additions — **ship this first**

Add to the existing developer intake form. New required section.

| Field | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Are environmental attributes conveyed under your offtake agreement? | Yes / No / Not sure | **Gating question.** "Not sure" → `unknown` |
| If retained, which program? | Select from `rec_programs` where `accepting_new_projects` | Filter by project jurisdiction |
| Registry | Auto-filled from program | Editable with warning |
| Generating unit ID | Text | Blank if not yet registered |
| Registry registration date | Date | Blank if not registered |
| Existing REC offtake contract? | Yes / No | If yes → counterparty, price, term, end date |
| Is any portion of future RECs already committed? | Yes / No | Critical for forward capacity |
| Revenue-grade meter installed? | Yes / No / Unknown | Determines whether program requirements are met |
| Interval data available? | Yes / No | Gates granular certificate optionality |

**The gating question is the most valuable field on the entire intake form.** It determines whether a REC product exists at all, and today it is not asked.

### 4.2 Screening Gate

Immediate, inline, on the intake form:

- **Conveyed** → REC module disabled. Banner: *"Environmental attributes for this project are conveyed to your power purchaser. Certificate revenue is not available on this project. Projected returns reflect energy revenue only."* Non-blocking — the project may still be a good equity deal.
- **Not sure** → *"We'll need to confirm this with your offtake agreement before modeling certificate revenue."* Creates a diligence task. Treated as conveyed until confirmed.
- **Retained** → REC module active.

### 4.3 REC Monetization Calculator

Extends the existing developer cost calculator.

Inputs: capacity, location, program (auto-suggested from jurisdiction), commissioning date, degradation.

Outputs: forecast annual certificate count, contracted or reference value with full provenance, 15-year projection, and a side-by-side of monetization paths:

| Path | Cash timing | Price certainty | Counterparty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spot sales as issued | Monthly, as issued | None — market | Varies |
| Forward strip at closing | Upfront or scheduled | Fixed for term | Named buyer |
| Program contract (Regime A) | Per program schedule | Fixed | Utility |

**The forward column is the pitch.** It shows a developer they can convert a volatile future revenue line into a contracted one at the same closing where they raise equity — one process, one set of counterparties, one set of fees.

Every output figure carries method, source, and `asof`. Regime B outputs carry the scenario band from § 3.4, never a point estimate presented as fact.

### 4.4 Variance Report Surface

Renders the Spec 13 § 7 report: forecast vs. issued by vintage month, cumulative variance, reason breakdown, downloadable PDF in EcoXchange design.

**This is a business development artifact, not just a dashboard.** Run it against a prospect's existing operating plant using their historical data and hand them a report showing certificates they may not have received. It is a concrete, self-evidently useful deliverable in a pipeline where you currently arrive with a concept — and it demonstrates the verification engine on the prospect's own asset before they have signed anything.

## § 5. LOI Clause Library

Extends the existing LOI builder. **[COUNSEL]** All clause text requires review before use in an executed document. These are drafting prompts, not final language.

### 5.1 Attribute Retention Representation

Developer represents that environmental attributes associated with project generation are retained by the project company and not conveyed under any offtake, interconnection, incentive, or financing agreement; and will disclose any existing or contemplated commitment of those attributes.

*Without this, everything downstream is built on an assumption.*

### 5.2 Registry Registration and Agency

Project company will register the generating unit with the applicable registry and appoint EcoXchange as its limited agent for the purposes of monitoring certificate issuance, reconciling issuance against verified generation, and — upon written instruction — effecting transfers. **Agency is limited and revocable. EcoXchange does not take title in the ordinary course.**

### 5.3 Verified Generation Data Access

Project company grants access to inverter telemetry, utility meter data, and interval data sufficient for production verification and certificate attribution, for the term of the agreement.

### 5.4 Forward Sale Consent

Project company consents to EcoXchange arranging forward sale of a specified portion of future certificates on terms subject to project company approval. **Approval right is retained by the developer** — a forward strip is a multi-year commitment of their asset's revenue and must not be arrangeable without their sign-off.

### 5.5 Exclusivity — narrow

Where included, exclusivity must be **limited to the specific program and vintage range contemplated**, time-boxed, and must not encumber the developer's other projects or their pre-existing broker relationships. Broad exclusivity in an LOI from a pre-revenue counterparty is a reason for a developer to decline, and rightly so.

### 5.6 REC Agency Authorization (separate document)

The standalone instrument referenced in `rec_registry_accounts.agency_authorization_ref`. Specifies: registry, account, scope of permitted actions, named authorized individuals, revocation mechanics, and record-keeping obligations. Registries and counterparties will ask for this. Draft it before you need it.

## § 6. Copy Library

Single locked module. Included in the CI vocabulary check.

**Approved terms:** production-verified · proprietary verification engine · three-source reconciliation · registry-issued certificates · forecast certificates · attribute attestation · contracted certificate revenue · merchant certificate revenue · target · modeled · estimated

**Prohibited, CI-enforced:** EcoXchange mints/issues/creates RECs · EcoXchange registry · guaranteed REC revenue · guaranteed delivery · carbon credits · carbon offsets · patent-pending · physics-verified · oracle · risk-free · insured · price forecast · expected 2027 price

**Always paired:** contracted **vs.** merchant · estimated figure **with** source and asof · yield **with** decomposition available · confidence band **with** its basis

## § 8. Acceptance Criteria

**Disclosure**

1. Attribute ownership card renders on every offering page and project dashboard, above the fold, non-dismissible.
2. `attribute_ownership = 'unknown'` renders variant B (conveyed) and logs a warning. No ambiguous state ever reaches a user.
3. Attribute copy strings are unmodifiable outside the locked module; CI fails on edits without review flag.

**Provenance**

4. No monetary figure renders outside `ProvenancedFigure`. Lint-enforced.
5. Every estimated figure displays source and `asof` **adjacent**, never tooltip-only.
6. Stale reference prices (>30 days) render `—` plus a stale notice, never a number.

**Decomposition**

7. Contracted and merchant are visually distinguishable without color alone — hatch pattern verified in greyscale and in a screenshot.
8. No blended yield appears in any view lacking its decomposition.
9. Sensitivity table always includes the zero-merchant row.
10. No trend line or projected future price renders anywhere.

**Developer**

11. Answering "Yes" to attribute conveyance immediately disables the REC module and shows the banner.
12. "Not sure" is treated as conveyed and creates a diligence task.
13. Calculator outputs carry method, source, and `asof` on every figure.
14. Variance report exports as a PDF in EcoXchange design tokens.

**Accessibility**

15. All REC panels pass WCAG AA contrast. The hatch pattern is not the sole differentiator for screen readers — `aria-label` distinguishes contracted from merchant.

**Vocabulary**

16. § 6 CI check passes across all components, copy, PDF templates, and test fixtures.

### 9.2 Tier 2 — one column pulled forward from Spec 13

**§ 3.1 Attribute Ownership Disclosure.** Do not defer this. It is the control preventing an investor from believing they hold an environmental claim they do not hold — a greenwashing exposure for EcoXchange and a fiduciary problem for any RIA who allocates.

It needs one standalone migration, not all of Spec 13: `project_rec_enrollment` with `attribute_ownership` as an enum (`retained` / `conveyed` / `retired_for_investors` / `unknown`), populated by the § 4.1 gating answer. Flag the migration in Spec 13's dependency notes so it is not created twice. If Spec 13 defines additional columns on this table, create them in the same migration rather than splitting it.

Build variants A and B only. Per § 3.1, **variant C must be absent from the codebase**, not merely unreachable.

### 9.3 Tier 3 — blocked on Spec 13

§ 3.2, § 3.3 (also Spec 15 § 6), § 3.4, § 3.5, § 4.3, § 4.4.

Do not build any Tier 3 surface with placeholder or illustrative data. A decomposition panel populated with invented splits is worse than no panel — it will end up in a screenshot.

§ 4.4 is the strongest business-development artifact in the spec set and it is firmly Tier 3. That is the cost of deferring Spec 13, and the argument for moving Spec 13 up the queue once Tiers 1 and 2 land.

### 9.4 Build order

1. **§ 6 copy library and CI vocabulary check.** First, not last. Every later surface validates against it; retrofitting is strictly more expensive.
2. **§ 7 primitives**, including the lint rule banning raw monetary rendering outside `ProvenancedFigure`. Without the lint rule the primitive is only a suggestion.
3. **§ 4.1 intake additions.** The "which program?" field selects from `rec_programs`, a Spec 13/14 artifact. Do not block on it — ship every other field and render the program select disabled with `PROGRAM SELECTION PENDING REGISTRY DATA` until the table is seeded. The gating question needs no table.
4. **§ 4.2 screening gate.** Confirm "Not sure" creates a diligence task and is treated as conveyed until confirmed. The conservative default must not be softened.
5. **Tier 2 migration, then § 3.1 card.** Locked copy module; verify the `unknown` → variant B fallback with build-time warning.
6. **§ 5 clause library**, plus the § 10 deny-list. Clauses as versioned data; **[COUNSEL]** watermark on any assembled document until clause status is approved.
7. **§ 5.6 agency authorization instrument.** Draft only, no integration.

Report and stop at the end of each step.

Precedents to mirror: `disclaimerConfig.ts` for the copy module shape; `YieldDisclosure.tsx` and `DataSourceAttribution.tsx` as `ProvenancedFigure`'s ancestors.

## § 10. LOI Securities Deny-List

§ 6's prohibited list governs REC vocabulary. It does not cover securities language in a generated LOI — a document can pass the § 6 check and still promise to raise capital by a date, or quote an IRR.

Extend the pre-render validation on assembled LOI documents to block:

- Any commitment or representation that EcoXchange **will** raise capital, or raise it by a stated date
- Any yield, IRR, distribution, or return figure — to the developer or to prospective investors
- Any characterization of the intended offering as registered, approved, or reviewed by a regulator
- Any reference to specific investors, investor demand, or committed capital
- Any binding obligation other than confidentiality and, where selected, the narrow exclusivity permitted by § 5.5

Implement as a **blocking pass that runs before render**, with a failing test per category, reachable before the renderer is. Reuse the § 6 CI machinery rather than building a second checker.

A non-binding LOI containing a capital-raising promise or a return figure stops being a harmless business document in a Reg D 506(c) context, and the author will not reliably catch it at 11pm.

17. Tier 1 ships and is demonstrable with `rec_programs` unseeded and Spec 13 unbuilt.
18. The Tier 2 migration creates `project_rec_enrollment` and is flagged in Spec 13's notes.
19. Variant C is absent from the codebase, not merely unreachable.
20. The § 10 deny-list blocks render on every category, with a failing test per category.
21. § 8.4's lint rule is verified by attempting a violation and confirming CI fails.
