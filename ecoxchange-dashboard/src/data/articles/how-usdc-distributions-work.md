Every month, the energy your project produced turns into dollars in your wallet —
specifically **USDC**, a fully-reserved US dollar stablecoin. Here is the path
from solar panel to payout.

## Why USDC

USDC is a digital dollar: one USDC is redeemable for one US dollar. Paying
distributions in USDC on the Base blockchain gives EcoXchange three things that
traditional ACH cannot:

- **Speed.** Settlement in minutes, not days.
- **Transparency.** Every distribution is an on-chain record you can audit.
- **Programmability.** Distributions can be split pro-rata across thousands of
  token holders automatically.

You will **not** manage private keys. Your wallet is embedded and handled for you;
USDC simply appears, and you can hold it or redeem it for dollars.

## The monthly flow

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Production | The project generates power under its PPA. |
| 2. Verification | The reconciliation engine confirms the month is verified. |
| 3. Calculation | Distributable revenue is computed per token. |
| 4. Settlement | USDC is sent pro-rata to every holder on Base. |

## A worked example

If you hold 100 of an offering's 25,000 tokens, you own 0.4%. When the project
distributes $14,580 of verified revenue for the month, your share is:

```
0.004 × $14,580 = $58.32 USDC
```

That payment only happens if the month is **verified**. A flagged month pays
nothing until it is resolved — which is exactly the protection you want.

## Cash out or reinvest

You choose, per holding, whether distributions are sent to your wallet (cash out)
or automatically used to buy more tokens (DRIP). You can change your preference at
any time from the dashboard.

depend on actual verified production and are not guaranteed.
