An **EcoXchange Solar Note (ESN)** is a fractional, tokenized ownership stake in a
real U.S. solar project. If you hold ESN tokens, you own a slice of the cash flows
that project generates — and you receive your share as monthly USDC distributions.

What makes it different from every other "invest in solar" product is one word:
**verified**.

## Projected vs. verified

Most solar offerings pay distributions based on *projected* production — a model
the developer built before the panels were installed. If the project
underperforms, you often find out late, if at all.

An ESN flips that. Distributions are triggered by **independently verified
production data**, reconciled from three sources (inverter, utility meter, and
satellite) before any money moves. You are paid on what the project *actually
did*, not what someone hoped it would do.

## What you actually own

- A fixed number of tokens representing your pro-rata share of one project.
- A claim on that project's monthly distributable revenue, paid in USDC.
- Full transparency: every verified month, with the underlying production, is
  visible on your dashboard.

## The lifecycle of a distribution

> Sunlight becomes kilowatt-hours, kilowatt-hours become verified production,
> verified production becomes USDC in your wallet.

1. The project produces power and sells it under a long-term PPA.
2. The reconciliation engine verifies the month's production.
3. Revenue is distributed pro-rata to every token holder in USDC.

## Who it is for

ESNs are offered under Reg D 506(c) to **accredited investors**. They suit people
who want contracted, infrastructure-backed income with real environmental impact —
and who would rather trust verified data than a developer's projection.
