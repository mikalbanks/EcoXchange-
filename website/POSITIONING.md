# EcoXchange website positioning

## Primary message

EcoXchange helps data-center operators and developers pursue an earlier path to power by coordinating distributed energy resources (DERs) and the partners needed to deploy them. Battery storage, onsite generation, and other suitable resources can support phased power access where site conditions, utility requirements, approvals, and economics permit.

The problem is delayed power access: long interconnection queues and grid upgrades can hold back data-center development and expansion. Capacity analysis is diligence in service of solving that problem, not the product promise.

## Present and future roles

- **Today:** EcoXchange qualifies sites, structures DER-enabled power pathways, and coordinates resource, utility, and deployment partners. Telemetry, verification, and finance readiness support delivery.
- **Future ambition:** EcoXchange aims to develop, own, operate, and deliver DER assets. Do not present those activities as current capabilities without evidence and approval.
- **Assessment:** The site intake is a customer-acquisition and diligence step to determine fit and next actions. It is not a standalone capacity-discovery product. Preserve its existing fields, Turnstile verification, and HubSpot contact/company flow.
- **Pilot:** Preserve the existing public pilot route and current implementation details until separately reviewed; the pilot is not the homepage's primary promise.

## Approved language

- Lead with **“A faster path to data-center power.”**
- Main CTA: **“Explore your power path”** linking to `/assessment`.
- Say DERs **can** create an earlier power phase or reduce dependence on long queues and upgrades **when feasible**; acknowledge site fit, utility and permitting requirements, approvals, and economics.
- Describe EcoXchange as coordinating deployment today, not as a consulting-only assessment service.

## Claims to avoid

- Guaranteed energization dates, MW, savings, queue bypass, or elimination of grid upgrades.
- Claims that EcoXchange currently owns, develops, operates, dispatches, or sells power assets unless substantiated and approved.
- Customer logos, project imagery, metrics, or case studies without permission and source evidence.
- Generated DER images portrayed as actual EcoXchange projects; caption them as illustrative.

## Design and rollout

The approved design reference is `design-reference-der-power-v2.png`: a light, product-led homepage with a conditional grid-versus-DER diagram, a single panoramic campus photo, an illustrative DER visual, and a later switchgear detail. Use Geist Sans, mineral-white surfaces, dark readable type, and EcoXchange green accents. Keep the React app, existing routes and assessment API fields, Turnstile, HubSpot contact/company path, and analytics event names. Verify locally before publishing.
