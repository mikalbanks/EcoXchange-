§ IV

How the fee is structured.

EcoXchange charges a 5% origination fee to the project SPV at offering close — paid from the capital raised for the project, not from investor returns. Investors pay no management fee, no annual platform fee, and no load charge.

After close, investor distributions are calculated entirely from verified production figures. The origination fee is a one-time transaction charge; it does not persist as an annual drag on yield. EcoXchange's incentive is to put capital to work — not to hold it.

The platform retains an ongoing servicing fee from the project SPV to cover distribution calculation, reporting, and audit delivery throughout the life of the offering. This is also borne by the project side, not by investors.
